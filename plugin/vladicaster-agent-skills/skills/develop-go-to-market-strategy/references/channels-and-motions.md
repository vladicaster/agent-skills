# Channels and Motions

Choose among founder-led, product-led, sales-led, partner-led, community-led, content-led, marketplace-led, and hybrid motions based on contract value, buying complexity, time to value, required education, and reachability.

Rank channels instead of listing them. For each selected channel, state:

- Target segment and funnel stage
- Message and offer
- Required assets and owner
- Cost and effort
- Expected learning
- Primary metric
- Stop, revise, and scale thresholds

Do not recommend paid acquisition until the offer, conversion path, instrumentation, economics, and delivery capacity are credible.
