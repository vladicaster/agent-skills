# Measurement and Experiments

Select measures appropriate to the model:

- Acquisition and qualified demand
- Activation and time to value
- Conversion and sales velocity
- Retention and engagement
- Expansion and referrals
- Revenue, margin, CAC hypothesis, and payback

Do not force SaaS metrics onto businesses where they do not fit.

Every experiment must state the hypothesis, audience, intervention, metric, baseline when known, success threshold, stop threshold, duration, owner, dependencies, and next decision. Prefer experiments that reduce an important uncertainty over activities that merely create volume.
