# Positioning and Messaging

Develop positioning from evidence:

> For **[specific customer]** who struggles with **[important problem]**, **[product]** is a **[category]** that delivers **[valuable outcome]**. Unlike **[alternative]**, it **[defensible difference]**.

Validate:

- The audience recognizes the category and problem.
- The outcome is valuable and supportable.
- The differentiation matters to the buying decision.
- Proof supports the claim.
- Terminology is clear rather than merely clever.

Create a core narrative plus segment-specific messages, reasons to believe, proof points, and objection responses. Mark unsupported claims as hypotheses.
