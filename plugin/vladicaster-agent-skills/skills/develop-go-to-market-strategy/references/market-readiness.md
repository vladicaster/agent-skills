# Market Readiness

Assess seven dimensions before recommending launch investment:

| Dimension | Key questions |
| --- | --- |
| Problem | Is it important, frequent, urgent, and currently underserved? |
| Product | Does the product credibly solve the promised problem? |
| Buyer | Are the user, economic buyer, evaluator, and blockers identifiable? |
| Proof | Are there results, demos, references, or credible evidence? |
| Delivery | Can the company onboard, support, and retain customers? |
| Offer | Is the value understandable and purchasable with acceptable risk? |
| Measurement | Can acquisition through retention be observed? |

Classify gaps as **launch blocker**, **near-term requirement**, **experiment**, **later enhancement**, or **not justified**. Do not prescribe demand generation as a substitute for missing product or delivery readiness.
