# Offers and Pricing

Design the offer as the complete path to value:

- Package and value metric
- Entry offer, trial, demo, pilot, or consultation
- Onboarding and time to first value
- Support and service boundary
- Risk reversal
- Retention and expansion path

Evaluate pricing against customer value, willingness-to-pay evidence, alternatives, margin, sales effort, support cost, and purchasing friction. Treat prices without evidence as hypotheses and define how to test them. Do not change live pricing without separate authorization.
