# Build-readiness model

Assess these gates: evidence quality, GTM approval, product boundary, PRD approval, traceability, nonfunctional requirements, risks and dependencies, validation plan, ownership, and engineering handoff.

Allowed states:

- **Ready**: approved and sufficient for engineering planning.
- **Conditionally ready**: work may proceed only under recorded conditions.
- **Blocked**: a material decision or dependency prevents handoff.
- **Not assessed**: evidence is insufficient to classify.

The overall state cannot exceed the weakest material gate. A condition or blocker must name an owner, next action, and target date when known.
