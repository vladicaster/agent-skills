# Launch Planning

Build phased plans:

1. **Foundation:** Close blockers, establish positioning, proof, offer, instrumentation, and ownership.
2. **Validation:** Run narrow tests with the beachhead segment and collect evidence.
3. **Expansion:** Scale validated channels and broaden only when capacity and economics support it.

For each phase define deliverables, owners, dependencies, budget assumptions, entry criteria, exit criteria, risks, and decision gates. A launch date without readiness criteria is not a launch plan.
