# World Repository Instructions

## Creative ownership

- Record the original creator or creators and do not imply that stewardship, editing, repository maintenance, or AI assistance transfers authorship.
- Preserve original source material separately from normalized canon and later proposals.
- Follow the repository's recorded source-interpretation and canon-approval authority.

## Canon and provenance

- Give consequential entries both a canon status and provenance when formal continuity requires it.
- Treat new material as Proposed unless an authorized decision establishes another status.
- Preserve disputed, unknown, and deprecated material when creative history or continuity depends on it.

## Changes

- Before revising canon, identify affected characters, locations, timelines, institutions, stories, adaptations, and artifacts.
- Update the canon register, affected entries, open questions or contradictions, and change history together.
- Do not silently rewrite preserved source notes or resolve ambiguity without authority.

## Validation and delivery

- Follow repository contribution, branch, approval, and review rules.
- Run applicable mechanical checks and report passed, failed, blocked, manual, and not-run results accurately.
- Review changes for broken links, duplicate identifiers, missing attribution, private material, and accidental promotion of proposals.
