# The Fabulous Unknown

## Episode One: The Impossible Chord

Graphic-novel script · First issue · 20 story pages

## Logline

In 2027, a broke glam-rock band accepts a humiliating bowling-alley gig, accidentally plays a chord that should not exist, and must learn to perform as a true ensemble before tiny interdimensional thieves dismantle the building—and their first audience.

## Canon established in this issue

The Fabulous Unknown consists of Astra Vane, Riff Divine, Velvet Voltage, and Crash Comet. Their powers emerge from the Impossible Chord and work best when they genuinely listen to one another. Their first transformation occurs during a performance at Galaxy Lanes. By the end of the issue, video of the incident has gone viral and an unknown intelligence has sent the message: “YOU PLAYED OUR CHORD.”

## Principal cast

**Astra Vane — Lead vocalist.** Cosmic-empress stage persona; meticulous and privately anxious. Her sung commands can affect people, objects, and physical forces. Metaphors can become dangerously literal.

**Riff Divine — Lead guitarist.** Magnificent, talented, and constitutionally unable to stop showing off. His solos cut through space and release cosmic energy.

**Velvet Voltage — Bassist.** Cool, practical, and the only member who reads contracts. Her bass frequencies manipulate gravity and mass.

**Crash Comet — Drummer.** Cheerful, impulsive, and powered by rhythm. His tempo bends time; missed beats produce unpredictable temporal glitches.

**Gordy Gold — Manager.** Possesses no supernatural abilities but regards management as the highest form of power. He can turn any catastrophe into a publicity opportunity.

## Visual language

The ordinary world should feel slightly faded, cramped, and practical. When the Impossible Chord appears, the palette erupts into ultraviolet, electric magenta, cyan, and gold. Powers look like extensions of performance—not generic energy blasts. The comedy should come from personality, timing, and consequences rather than making the characters incompetent.

## Page 1 — Cold open

**Full-page splash**

Galaxy Lanes is coming apart in spectacular slow motion. Bowling balls orbit the stage like planets. A glowing rupture tears open the ceiling above lane twelve. Tiny glitter-covered creatures flee with shoes, trophies, and a nacho-cheese dispenser. The Fabulous Unknown stand transformed in impossible glam costumes.

Astra points toward the rupture like an empress commanding a fleet. Riff is mid-solo. Velvet anchors the group as gravity bends around her. Crash raises both sticks over a drum kit surrounded by concentric clocks.

**Caption:** Six minutes earlier, The Fabulous Unknown were not superheroes.

**Astra:** From the bridge! And this time—listen to each other!

**Title:** The Fabulous Unknown

**Subtitle:** The Impossible Chord

## Page 2 — Above the laundromat

**Panel 1**

Exterior, late afternoon. A tired laundromat occupies the ground floor of an aging Los Angeles strip mall. Above it, one window glows purple. A handmade banner hangs crookedly: THE FABULOUS UNKNOWN — WORLD HEADQUARTERS.

**Caption:** Earlier that day.

**Panel 2**

Inside the cramped rehearsal room. Costumes, cables, unpaid bills, and laundry baskets compete for space. Astra rehearses a grand entrance while Velvet studies a utility shutoff notice.

**Astra:** Darkness. One beam of silver light. Then I descend from the heavens.

**Velvet:** The heavens owe the electric company four hundred and twelve dollars.

**Panel 3**

Riff holds a heroic pose before a full-length mirror.

**Riff:** Art has never been constrained by utility billing.

**Velvet:** Art is currently using three space heaters.

**Panel 4**

Crash enters carrying four coffees and no drumsticks.

**Crash:** Good news. I remembered everyone’s order.

**Astra:** Where are your sticks?

**Crash:** I knew there was a fifth thing.

## Page 3 — The booking

**Panel 1**

Gordy Gold bursts through the door in a gold jacket, carrying a contract and the confidence of a man who has not read it.

**Gordy:** Cancel your evening. Destiny called.

**Velvet:** Did destiny leave a deposit?

**Panel 2**

Gordy reveals a glossy flyer: GALAXY LANES — COSMIC BOWL NIGHT. The pictured “stage” is a carpeted platform between the arcade and shoe counter.

**Gordy:** Industry showcase. Packed house. The right people will be watching.

**Riff:** Which people?

**Gordy:** People with influence. People with disposable income. People in league play.

**Panel 3**

Astra stares at the flyer.

**Astra:** You booked us at a bowling alley.

**Gordy:** I booked you at a venue with lanes of opportunity.

**Panel 4**

Velvet points to the fee.

**Velvet:** This barely covers gas.

**Gordy:** Then tonight, we travel as artists.

## Page 4 — Galaxy Lanes

**Panel 1**

The band’s battered van arrives beneath a giant neon bowling pin. Its side reads ONE NIGHT ONLY: WORLDWIDE, with “WORLDWIDE” squeezed above a dent.

**Panel 2**

Inside, a children’s birthday party occupies half the room. Bowlers ignore the stage. A mechanical galaxy spins above the lanes.

**Astra:** Gordy, the “packed house” is eleven league bowlers and a birthday party.

**Gordy:** Demographically diverse.

**Panel 3**

Riff inspects a battered amplifier already onstage. A tarnished metal plate bears an unfamiliar spiral symbol.

**Riff:** This amplifier has character.

**Velvet:** That is corrosion.

**Panel 4**

Crash taps the house drum kit. A cymbal falls off.

**Crash:** It likes me.

## Page 5 — Soundcheck

**Panel 1**

A teenage employee named Nia adjusts a tiny mixing console while wearing rental shoes.

**Nia:** Management says you have to stop by ten. Cosmic Bowl starts at ten-oh-five.

**Astra:** We are Cosmic Bowl.

**Nia:** No. Cosmic Bowl is when we turn the lights off.

**Panel 2**

Riff strikes a chord. The old amplifier emits a strange harmonic. Somewhere inside it, a pinprick of starlight appears.

**SFX:** VVVVOMM

**Riff:** Hear that? Vintage warmth.

**Velvet:** I heard a microwave asking for help.

**Panel 3**

Crash counts in too quickly. Astra misses her entrance. Riff continues soloing.

**Astra:** Stop. We are not four simultaneous solo acts.

**Riff:** Some of us are naturally more simultaneous than others.

**Panel 4**

Velvet plucks one low note. Every bowling ball on the return trembles almost imperceptibly.

**Velvet:** Did anyone else—

**Gordy (off-panel):** Two minutes to transcendence!

## Page 6 — The show begins

**Panel 1**

The Fabulous Unknown take the stage in ambitious but clearly homemade glam costumes. Astra raises both arms.

**Astra:** Citizens of Galaxy Lanes—

**Bowler:** You’re blocking lane nine!

**Panel 2**

They launch into their opening song, “Born Apologizing.” The first bars are messy. Crash rushes. Riff overplays. Astra forces the melody forward. Velvet watches all three.

**Panel 3**

A child from the birthday party becomes fascinated. A few adults raise phones.

**Child:** Are they famous?

**Parent:** Not at these prices.

**Panel 4**

Inside Riff’s amplifier, the pinprick of light grows into a geometric star. Four faint waveforms reach toward the band members.

## Page 7 — Breakdown

**Panel 1**

The song reaches its bridge. Astra signals for everyone to pull back. Riff interprets this as a request for a longer solo.

**Astra:** Riff!

**Riff:** I am resolving it!

**Velvet:** You left the song eight bars ago.

**Panel 2**

Crash tries to follow Riff and knocks over a tom. The music collapses. A bowler rolls a strike during the silence.

**SFX:** KRAK-KRAK-KRAK!

**Bowler:** That’s timing.

**Panel 3**

Astra turns away from the audience, furious but controlled.

**Astra:** From the bridge. Watch me. Listen to Velvet. Crash—breathe.

**Crash:** While drumming?

**Astra:** Selectively.

**Panel 4**

Velvet looks at each member.

**Velvet:** Nobody wins this one alone.

## Page 8 — The Impossible Chord

**Panel 1**

Close on Crash. He closes his eyes and begins a steady, restrained pulse.

**SFX:** THUM. THUM. THUM. THUM.

**Panel 2**

Velvet enters exactly between the beats. The room seems to become heavier, quieter, attentive.

**Panel 3**

Riff lowers his volume and answers Velvet’s phrase instead of overpowering it. For once, he smiles at another musician rather than the audience.

**Panel 4**

Astra hears the shape they are creating. She sings a note that does not belong to any familiar scale.

**Astra:** Ahhh—

**Panel 5**

Their four sounds meet. The panel geometry fractures into a luminous cosmic chord.

**SFX:** THE IMPOSSIBLE CHORD

No conventional lettering can fully contain it. The sound should be visual: four distinct colors forming something new at their intersection.

## Page 9 — Transformation

**Full-page splash**

The chord explodes through the band. Their homemade costumes transform into magnificent cosmic glam regalia. Astra wears a radiant stellar crown. Riff’s guitar becomes a blade-like constellation. Velvet’s bass contains a moving galaxy. Crash’s drums orbit him in fragments of synchronized time.

The ceiling opens above lane twelve—not as destruction, but as a perfect circular aperture revealing impossible stars.

Audience phones all display the same unfamiliar spiral.

**Astra:** Did we just become better dressed?

**Riff:** Finally, the universe demonstrates taste.

## Page 10 — Uninvited guests

**Panel 1**

Small creatures tumble from the rupture. They are colorful, sharp-toothed, and covered in natural glitter. One lands inside a bowling shoe.

**Crash:** Fans?

**Velvet:** Fans usually enter through the front.

**Panel 2**

The creatures swarm the shoe counter, snatching anything shiny.

**Nia:** Hey! Those laces are inventoried!

**Panel 3**

One creature tears the Galaxy Lanes championship trophy from its case. Another drags away the nacho-cheese dispenser.

**Gordy:** Nobody panic. Exclusive merchandise opportunity.

**Panel 4**

A giant creature peers through the rupture. Only one enormous eye and several jeweled claws are visible.

**Astra:** Finish the song.

## Page 11 — First powers

**Panel 1**

Astra sings instinctively while pointing at the fleeing creatures.

**Astra (singing):** Hold everything—

**Panel 2**

Every loose object in the room freezes in midair: shoes, pins, cheese, people’s drinks—and Riff’s hair.

**Riff:** Release my volume immediately.

**Panel 3**

The creatures are unaffected and continue running between frozen objects.

**Velvet:** Your command was metaphorical.

**Astra:** Apparently the universe is a literalist.

**Panel 4**

Crash attempts a dramatic fill. Time lurches. A thrown bowling ball reverses into a bowler’s hand, then rolls forward again.

**Bowler:** I demand that count twice.

## Page 12 — Gravity lesson

**Panel 1**

Velvet hits a deep descending bass line. The creatures suddenly become too heavy to move, flattening harmlessly against the carpet.

**SFX:** BWOOOM

**Velvet:** That works.

**Panel 2**

She bends the note too far. The audience, furniture, and band all become heavy. Riff’s platform boots punch through the stage.

**Riff:** The floor has betrayed me!

**Panel 3**

Velvet corrects the pitch. Gravity releases abruptly. Bowling balls, people, and creatures float upward.

**Velvet:** Correction: learning.

**Panel 4**

A creature sails past Gordy carrying his wallet.

**Gordy:** That one has representation!

## Page 13 — Riff cuts loose

**Panel 1**

Riff strikes a heroic pose and begins a blistering solo.

**Riff:** Stand back. Space requires editing.

**Panel 2**

A bright guitar phrase slices open a portal across lanes seven and eight. Through it, we see a bewildered wedding reception somewhere else.

**Wedding guest:** Is this the entertainment?

**Panel 3**

Several creatures dive through. Riff grins.

**Riff:** Controlled evacuation.

**Panel 4**

The portal widens toward the birthday table. Astra grabs Riff’s shoulder.

**Astra:** You are not controlling anything!

**Riff:** Control is aesthetically limiting.

## Page 14 — Off the beat

**Panel 1**

The giant jeweled claw reaches farther through the ceiling rupture. It begins pulling the entire building upward.

**Panel 2**

Crash sees a child drifting toward the opening. He plays faster to reach her.

**Panel 3**

Time accelerates around him. Birthday candles burn down instantly. Gordy’s phone cycles through hundreds of notifications. A nearby teenager briefly appears middle-aged, then snaps back.

**Gordy:** We are trending in several possible futures!

**Panel 4**

Crash slows the beat and catches the child safely on a cymbal.

**Child:** Again!

**Crash:** Absolutely not until I understand it.

## Page 15 — The band falls apart

**Panel 1**

All four powers collide. Gravity bends sideways. Portals flicker. Frozen drinks resume splashing. Time skips. The building rises several feet.

**Panel 2**

Astra shouts over the chaos.

**Astra:** Stop playing!

**Panel 3**

They stop. Their transformed costumes flicker. The powers fade—but the rupture remains, and the giant creature continues pulling itself through.

**Riff:** I would like the record to show that stopping made it worse.

**Panel 4**

Velvet studies the four fading colors around them.

**Velvet:** The chord wasn’t four notes played together.

**Panel 5**

She looks directly at the others.

**Velvet:** It was four people listening.

## Page 16 — Finding the song

**Panel 1**

The band huddles while creatures swarm around their boots.

**Astra:** Crash, hold us steady. Velvet, tell us where the room is pulling. Riff—

**Riff:** Less?

**Astra:** Answer us.

**Panel 2**

Crash begins the same restrained pulse from soundcheck.

**SFX:** THUM. THUM. THUM. THUM.

**Panel 3**

Velvet plays a bass line that stabilizes gravity. Floating audience members drift gently toward the floor.

**Panel 4**

Riff begins a melodic response. His smaller portals line up into a path leading back toward the ceiling rupture.

**Panel 5**

Astra smiles—not performing confidence, but feeling it.

**Astra:** From the bridge.

## Page 17 — The Fabulous Unknown

**Full-page ensemble splash**

They play as one band. Their four power colors interweave without losing their individuality. Crash creates a steady corridor of slowed time. Velvet curves gravity toward the rupture. Riff shapes space into a luminous ramp. Astra sings a command that is both lyric and invitation.

**Astra (singing):** TAKE WHAT YOU BROUGHT—AND GO HOME IN GLORY!

The creatures cheer, grab their stolen objects, and surf the combined wave back through the opening. Even the nacho-cheese dispenser ascends heroically.

## Page 18 — Encore

**Panel 1**

The giant creature’s eye narrows. For a moment, it appears angry.

**Panel 2**

Then it notices the championship trophy. Its eye widens with delight. A claw takes the trophy and withdraws.

**Panel 3**

The band resolves the song on a final, controlled version of the Impossible Chord. The rupture seals into a single glittering point.

**SFX:** VMMMM—TING.

**Panel 4**

Silence. The band’s costumes return to their handmade forms. One bowling pin falls over.

**SFX:** tok.

**Panel 5**

The birthday child raises both hands.

**Child:** ENCORE!

## Page 19 — Going viral

**Panel 1**

Morning. The band sits exhausted in their rehearsal room above the laundromat. News clips and social videos fill every screen.

**Headline:** GLAM BAND DESTROYS BOWLING ALLEY, SAVES MAYBE 40 PEOPLE

**Panel 2**

Gordy paces with three phones.

**Gordy:** Two labels. Four networks. A breakfast cereal. The city’s insurance investigator.

**Velvet:** Which one called first?

**Gordy:** The insurance investigator, but his offer lacks vision.

**Panel 3**

Riff studies footage of his transformed self.

**Riff:** The camera understood me.

**Astra:** We nearly pulled a building into space.

**Crash:** But we found the tempo.

**Panel 4**

Velvet receives an email and turns the laptop around.

**Velvet:** Galaxy Lanes found their vision.

**On screen:** NOTICE OF CLAIM — ESTIMATED DAMAGES: $412,000

**Panel 5**

Astra notices the amount.

**Astra:** That is exactly one thousand electric bills.

## Page 20 — The answer

**Panel 1**

Night. The rehearsal room is empty except for Crash, who has returned to retrieve his forgotten drumsticks.

**Panel 2**

His bass drum gives one soft pulse by itself.

**SFX:** THUM.

**Panel 3**

The unfamiliar spiral symbol appears across the drumhead in ultraviolet light.

**Panel 4**

Letters form beneath it.

**Message:** YOU PLAYED OUR CHORD.

**Panel 5**

Close on Crash, delighted rather than frightened.

**Crash:** Guys? I think we have fans.

**Final caption:** Next: “Management Is a Superpower”

## End of Episode One

## Continuity notes

- The identity of the sender remains Unknown and is not yet canon.
- The Impossible Chord requires mutual attention, not mere technical synchronization.
- Recorded footage may carry a faint supernatural charge; this is available for later development but is not demonstrated conclusively here.
- Galaxy Lanes, Nia, the birthday child, and the displaced wedding reception can recur.
- The band is now publicly associated with the incident, but “superhero” is not yet a legal or universally accepted designation.
