# Quality checklist

Record each item as **passed**, **failed**, **manual**, or **not run**. A generated file existing is not proof that its content is correct.

## Story and geography

- The opening establishes who, where, and what is happening.
- Each beat visibly motivates the next.
- Remote discovery occurs in a location that cannot be confused with the destination.
- Arrival and payoff are legible without relying solely on captions or narration.

## Continuity

- Face, hair, body, age, wardrobe, and accessories remain stable.
- Exterior and interior geometry agree with assigned references.
- Props persist, remain in the correct hand, and do not duplicate.
- Light and time progress intentionally.
- Brand colors and generic environmental signs remain within constraints.

## Physics and camera

- Hands contact objects without fusion or unexplained teleportation.
- Doors rotate around a plausible hinge and clear the subject's path.
- Exchanges show a consistent giver, receiver, and object trajectory.
- Walking direction, eyelines, reflections, shadows, and scale are plausible.
- Camera motion does not conceal a failed essential action.

## Audio

- Dialogue matches the visible speaker and intended language.
- Brand names, domains, initials, and numbers are pronounced as intended.
- Ambience and music do not mask speech or create an unintended tonal shift.
- No accidental narration, duplicated words, or abrupt audio cutoff is present.

## Typography and finishing

- Captions come from verified text and match the video's actual audio timing.
- Product name, URL, call to action, and end-card spelling are exact.
- Generated scenery does not contain misleading or malformed readable text.
- Caption contrast, line length, margins, and duration are legible on a phone.
- End-card text is within title-safe areas and remains visible long enough.

## Format and delivery

- Width, height, aspect ratio, duration, frame rate, codec, and audio stream match delivery requirements.
- Landscape and vertical versions use native compositions.
- Reels/Shorts interface-safe zones do not cover essential action or text.
- The delivered file or media link opens and corresponds to the reviewed version.
- Job status and generation cost are recorded from provider evidence.

## Repair decision

Regenerate for broken causality, identity, location, essential action, or severe physics. Finish deterministically for exact text, captions, end cards, simple overlays, trims, or replaceable audio. Accept only variations that preserve meaning, continuity, brand, and platform fit.
